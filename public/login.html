What is an Access Code?

Your teacher generates a unique code for you when setting up your account. It looks like FE-A-36 or SE-B-101. This code contains your academic details — year, division, and roll number.

Format: YearCode-Division-RollNumber

Don't have a code? Contact your teacher or class coordinator.
